## Visual language

- The software product is **at 1801** and its visual wordmark is **at 1801.** Always lowercase. Keep **Sandikala Studio** as the creator and as the service provider in client-facing proposals. Product copy lives in `src/lib/product.ts`.
- Use the existing tokens in `src/styles/main.css`: `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--paper`, `--white`, `--accent`, `--sage`, and `--dark`. The palette is warm off-white, charcoal, and restrained terracotta or sage accents.
- Use `--display` (Newsreader) for editorial headings, `--sans` (Manrope) for labels and controls, and `--body` (DM Sans) for body text. The workspace uses compact 10–13px table and control text, with larger display headings.
- Spacing is mostly custom rather than a formal token scale. Existing layouts use roughly 8–20px gaps and 20–40px section padding; keep new workspace content in a centered container.
- Borders use `--line` and 1px strokes. Corners are square or about 2px on controls; pills are reserved for status. Shadows are limited to overlays.
- Keep the authenticated workspace quiet: no gradients, dashboard cards, sidebar, or decorative motion. Let typography, whitespace, and row dividers establish hierarchy.
